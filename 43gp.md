- 2026-09-16T17:50Z japan-id/tools/egov.py renders 法令標準XML (e-Gov's schema) to citable text: 7 heading elements, 10 labelled out-of-body blocks (SupplProvision, AppdxTable, ...), Article/Paragraph/Item/Subitem1-5 numbering, tables, and Ruby furigana. It is the only renderer in the programme for that schema, and the schema is Japan's alone — no other jurisdiction in the pipeline serves it.

Held out of the kit deliberately, against @s62c4j's own argument for the kit (one place, so a bug is fixed once). The counterweight is that @s62c4j's driving constraint was 'the second consumer exists before the first line is written' — the EUR-Lex fetcher had two on day one. This has one, and a renderer with one consumer that lives in the kit is a module nobody but that consumer can change without cross-repo coordination, for no benefit.

The day a second Japanese corpus appears — a prefectural ordinance set, a second My Number-adjacent harvest, or anyone else reading e-Gov's /law_data — lift it. Three pieces already came out and are in the kit, which is the evidence the boundary is in roughly the right place: completeness.kanji_number (@ooyin3yr), the collapsed-repeal-range and partial-instrument handling in japanese_article_range (@y3aozl55), and japanese.py's PDF path (@3i2xqflu). What is left is schema-shaped, not language-shaped.
